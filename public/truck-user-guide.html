How to Order from The Turn Truck

A friendly guide for members · 5 easy steps

Step by step

1

Choose how you're paying

At the top of the page, choose Member Account to charge to your account, or Guest – Pay at Pickup if you'll pay by card when you collect your order.

Payment

Member Account
Charge this order to a verified member account.
Guest – Pay at Pickup
A credit card must be provided at pickup before the order is released.
2

Enter your account information

Fill in your first and last name, your 4–6 digit member number, and your mobile number. Your mobile is required so staff can reach you with any questions.

3

Browse the menu and add items

Use the category dropdown or swipe the row to filter. Items with simple choices use + / − directly. Items needing customization (bread, toppings) show a Customize button.

4

Review your order and add a tip

Scroll to the bottom to see Your Truck Order. Choose a tip — 18%, 20%, 22%, custom, or no tip. Add any allergy or special instructions in the notes field.

5

Submit and head to the truck

Tap Submit Truck Order. Your order is charged directly to your member account — no cash or card needed. Head to the truck for pickup when it's ready.

Submit
I authorize this food truck order to be charged or reconciled to the account listed above.
Submit Truck Order

Already ordered? Check your status

Scroll to the bottom of the page and tap Check Truck Order. Enter your member number — and your order number too if you have it (it looks like 5001). Tap Check Status to see where your order is.
